HOW WE CAN HELP

We are very flexible, and we can work with you to tailor our offering to suit your specific needs.
Ask us about what makes our offering so different, and how we deliver great results while remaining highly cost effective!

Advertising

Recommend appropriate media for advertising to identify active candidates using traditional and digital channels.

Talent Search

Find and contact passive candidates with niche or bespoke ‘hard-to-find’ skill-sets.

Short-listing

Identify candidates closely matching the vacancy’s criteria, interview then present short-listed candidates for a client interview.

Interviewing

Facilitate client candidate interviews: interview scheduling, question design, running the interview and managing the results. Recommending the next steps.

Background Checks

Obtain verbal references from previous employers. Ministry of Justice checks, verification of relevant qualifications’ and Visa checks.

Testing & Assessment

Provide access to a variety of skills and personality testing tools to assist decision making.

Contract Negotiation

Manage the process of presentation of offers and ensuring a smooth process with no surprises through to employment.

Candidate Management

Ensure successful and unsuccessful applicants / candidates are notified about their application and its outcome.

Recruitment Process Coaching

Assisting clients to build their own recruitment strategy and processes.
